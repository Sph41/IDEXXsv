## Competitive Matrix

*Full research, pricing, and sources: [competitive-matrix.md](competitive-matrix.md)*

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness | Implication |
|------------|----------|----------|-------------|
| Duolingo | Streak repair is genuinely emotional/social — free-earned Gems, app-initiated friend gifting, daily celebrations | Recovery mechanics prevent/undo loss but never acknowledge the return moment itself | Bar is higher than "add a freeze" — Streakly needs the comeback moment itself, which even Duolingo doesn't design |
| Babbel | Structured curriculum keeps non-gamified learners engaged | No streak-freeze/repair at all; a break is just a reset | Confirms this gap is category-wide, not just Streakly's |
| Elevate | Achievement unlocks + progress calendar for motivation ("5 min/day" positioning closest to Streakly) | No forgiveness mechanic found | Closest positioning analog, but leaves the same gap open |
| Memrise | Pivoted from user-generated content to produced video, ~2x session length | Streak mechanic itself is basic (plant metaphor); dropped community content along the way | Content quality can drive engagement, but doesn't replace a recovery experience |
| Brilliant | AI tutor (Koji) as a personalization-driven retention lever | No streak-freeze/comeback mechanic found | Shows an alternative retention lever (AI guidance) worth watching, not a direct answer to the streak-break problem |

**White space:** (1) No competitor designs the return-after-a-break moment as an acknowledgment — only as loss-prevention. (2) No competitor addresses pre-break streak anxiety in new users, only reactive fixes after a miss.
