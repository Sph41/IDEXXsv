# NPS Feedback Findings — Streak & Retention

*Source: 10 raw NPS comments. Prepared for Marcus.*

## Themes Mentioned More Than Once (Ranked by Frequency)

### 1. Streak breaks trigger permanent loss with no way to recover — 6 mentions

- "I hit a 20-day streak, missed one day, and it reset to zero. I haven't opened the app since. Felt pointless to start over."
- "I want Streakly to feel like a coach that helps me get back on track, not a scorekeeper that punishes me for missing a day."
- "I broke my streak once and there was no way to recover it. Other apps let you freeze a streak. Why not this one?"
- "I wish it would make coming back easier instead of making me feel like I failed and have to start from scratch."
- "Deleted after 3 weeks. The moment I lost my streak the whole thing lost its meaning."
- "The streak is the only thing keeping me engaged, but the second I lost it, I was done."

### 2. Notifications feel naggy or random, leading users to disable them — 2 mentions

- "The first week was genuinely fun. After that the daily reminder just started to feel like nagging."
- "The notifications feel random. I got three in one afternoon and just turned them all off."

### 3. The app doesn't acknowledge the user's current state — 2 mentions

- "I want Streakly to feel like a coach that helps me get back on track, not a scorekeeper that punishes me for missing a day."
- "The home screen looks the same whether I'm on a 2-day streak or coming back after two weeks away. Nothing acknowledges where I am."

*Note: one comment stood alone but reinforces theme 1 — "Love the lessons. I just forget it exists after a couple of days. If it pulled me back with something useful I'd come back." Not counted as a ranked theme (single mention), but it's evidence that users want to be pulled back in, not just left alone after disengaging.*

## Praise vs. Complaints

**Praise (2 of 10):**
- "The first week was genuinely fun." *(qualified — see complaint below)*
- "Love the lessons."

**Complaints (8 of 10):**
- Streak resets to zero with no recovery (5 comments)
- Notifications feel naggy or random (2 comments)
- No acknowledgment of current state / desire for a "coach" feel (1 comment, overlapping with above)
- Forgotten/no re-engagement pull after disengaging (1 comment)

Both product strengths users volunteered (first week, lesson content) are undercut in the same breath by a complaint — the content isn't the problem, what happens around it is.

## Top 3 Actionable Issues

1. **No recovery mechanism after a streak break.** The single most repeated and most specific ask: users explicitly want a streak freeze or equivalent (one names a competitor by feature), and the lack of one is directly tied to the two uninstall/abandonment stories in this batch.
2. **Notification strategy is punitive and untuned.** Two independent complaints — one about tone (nagging), one about volume/relevance (three in one afternoon) — both ending in the user opting out entirely. This is a second, separate failure point from the reset issue, not the same problem restated.
3. **No state-aware acknowledgment on return.** The home screen treats a 2-day streak and a return-after-two-weeks user identically. Users are asking the product to recognize where they are, not just record a number — this is the mechanism that would let the app "pull them back with something useful" instead of relying on generic reminders.

## Bottom Line for Marcus

All three issues point at the same underlying gap: the product has no designed moment for "you slipped, here's what happens now." Recovery, notifications, and state-aware acknowledgment are three different touchpoints for the same missing piece.
